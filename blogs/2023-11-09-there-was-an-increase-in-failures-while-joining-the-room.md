Nov 9 , 07:50 UTC Resolved - There was an observed increase in failures when attempting to join the room from the EU region between 1:20 PM IST and 2:00 PM IST on November 9, 2023. This issue arose due to the primary cluster in the EU region being under maintenance, resulting in the temporary rerouting of traffic to a backup cluster. Although originally planned as a zero-downtime activity, an oversight in our standard operating procedure led to an unplanned 30-minute downtime.
